## SELECTED DESIGN: "Florida Industrial Pro"

Intentionally different from every dark-navy-orange site in the agency portfolio.
Looks like a real working trade shop's catalog — approachable, professional, product-forward.
Reference: Northern Tool + MPWSR layout approach.

### Design Movement
**American Industrial Catalog** — Grainger meets a Florida trade shop. Clean, functional, zero decoration for decoration's sake. Every element earns its place.

### Color System (NO dark navy, NO amber/orange)
- **Page background:** `#F2EFE9` — warm concrete (not pure white, not grey)
- **Card/product surface:** `#FFFFFF` — pure white so products pop
- **Nav + Footer:** `#1C1C1C` — near-black, strong and grounded
- **Primary text:** `#1C1C1C` — near-black
- **Secondary text:** `#6B7280` — steel grey
- **Construction Yellow (PRIMARY ACCENT):** `#FFD100` — ANSI safety yellow, all primary CTAs
- **Signal Red (SERVICE ACCENT):** `#CC2020` — service/repair/warranty badges and CTAs
- **Yellow hover:** `#E6C200` — slightly darker yellow for hover states
- **Border:** `#E5E0D8` — warm light grey border
- **Section divider:** `#2D2D2D` — dark grey for alternating sections

### Typography System
- **Display/H1/H2:** `Barlow Condensed` ExtraBold 800 — compressed, industrial, authoritative
- **H3/Subheads:** `Barlow` SemiBold 600
- **Body:** `Inter` 400/500 — clean, readable
- **Product names:** `Barlow` Bold 700
- **Labels/badges:** `Barlow Condensed` Bold 700 uppercase

### Layout Paradigm
**Product-grid-first homepage** — products appear above the fold, no scrolling required.
- Full-width hero with product image RIGHT, headline LEFT
- Immediately below: 8 category tiles in a 4x2 grid
- Then: "Featured Products" with real product cards (4 per row desktop)
- Left-rail category nav on product pages (desktop), tab filter on mobile
- NO hero carousels, NO testimonials above fold, NO centered text walls

### Signature Elements
1. **Yellow left-border accent** on featured product cards and service callouts
2. **Red "AUTHORIZED SERVICE CENTER" badge** on warranty/repair items
3. **Category tiles** — concrete background, large icon, yellow hover state

### Brand Voice
- Headlines: "The Pump You Need. In Stock Today."
- CTAs: "Call for Pricing" / "Get a Quote" / "Schedule Service" — never "Learn More"
- Tagline: "Sarasota's Industrial Equipment Specialists Since 1990"
