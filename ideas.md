## SELECTED DESIGN: "Steel & Precision"

### Design Movement
Premium Industrial / Heavy Equipment Dealer — think Parker Hannifin, Graco, or Cat dealer sites.
Dark navy-to-charcoal base. Steel blue accents. Amber CTAs. Authoritative and data-forward.

### Color System
- **Page background:** #0F1923 (deep navy-black)
- **Section surfaces:** #162030 (dark navy)
- **Card surfaces:** #1C2B3A (steel navy)
- **Primary CTA:** #F59E0B (amber gold)
- **Steel blue accent:** #3B82F6 (bright blue — icons, badges, links)
- **Alert/emergency:** #EF4444 (red)
- **Text primary:** #F1F5F9 (near-white)
- **Text secondary:** #94A3B8 (muted slate)
- **Border:** #1E3A5F (dark blue border)
- **Hover surface:** #243447

### Typography
- **Display/H1/H2:** Oswald 700 — compressed, industrial authority
- **H3/Labels:** Oswald 500 uppercase
- **Body:** Inter 400/500 — clean, readable
- **Accent numbers:** Oswald 700 tabular

### Layout Principles
- Full-width dark sections with subtle diagonal dividers
- Asymmetric hero: headline left, equipment image right
- Product cards in 3-4 column grids with amber hover borders
- Sticky nav with phone number and amber CTA always visible
- Section headers always left-aligned with amber underline accent
- NO centered text walls
